orders
   order_id            PK                               varchar
   order_timestamp                                      timestamptz
   channel                                              varchar
   delivery_zone       (can be empty)                   integer
   amount_original                                      decimal(12,2)
   currency_original                                    varchar(3)
   amount_eur                                           decimal(12,2)
   original_order_id   -> orders (can be empty)         varchar

   delivery_timestamp                                  timestamptz
   level                                               varchar
   event_type                                          varchar
   order_id            -> orders (can be empty)        varchar
   driver_id                                           varchar
   app_version                                         varchar
   battery                                             integer
   raw_message                                         varchar

   registered_at                            timestamptz
   loyalty_tier                             varchar
